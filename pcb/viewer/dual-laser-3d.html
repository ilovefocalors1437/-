The two laser channels

LD1 · 660 nm red

Shallower-penetrating visible red channel. Design intent: help local blood flow in the foot.

LD2 · 850 nm near-infrared

Deeper-penetrating channel aimed at the foot fascia. Design intent: help loosen the hyaluronan (HA) layer in densified fascia.

What this is, and what it is not

  • This board is a mock reconstruction made to stand in for the user's original design file. The real schematic, part choices and layout may differ in every detail.
  • It is generated by a script (KiCad 7 pcbnew API), routed by a small grid router, and checked with KiCad's DRC: 0 violations, 0 unconnected items. It has not been built, simulated or bench-tested.
  • Laser safety: a ~100 mW-class diode is Class 3B. 850 nm is invisible, so there is no blink reflex to protect the eye. A real device needs the hardware interlock, wavelength-rated eyewear and a regulatory review before use on people.
